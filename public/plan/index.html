        Questions

        Where does the climate data come from?

        The free Open-Meteo historical archive: daily weather for the last five years at your coordinates. Frost dates are the median last spring and first fall days at or below 0 °C (32 °F). The hardiness zone comes from the average coldest night of the year.

        Does it cost anything?

        No. Common Ground is a free community tool. There's no account and nothing to upgrade.

        Where is my plan stored?

        In this browser's local storage, on this device. Share links contain the plan itself, so nothing is uploaded unless you send the link to someone.

        What is a guild?

        Plants that help each other: one fixes nitrogen, another draws pollinators, another confuses pests or shades the soil. The classic is the Three Sisters: corn, beans and squash.

        The Bay is frost-free. Why do I see frost dates?

        Most of the flatlands rarely freeze, but the hills, Walnut Creek and parts of the South Bay do. If your site comes back frost-free, the calendar times plantings around the cool and warm seasons instead.

        Can I adjust my frost dates?

        Yes. After a site check, drag the frost-date sliders. The calendar redraws as you go, which helps if your yard is a cold pocket or sits against a warm wall.
