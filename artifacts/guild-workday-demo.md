## Agenda

- 8:45 a.m. — Set out tools, water, and welcome signs
- 9:45 a.m. — Arrival · nametags, welcome, and housekeeping
- 10:15 a.m. — Introductions · what are we planting, nurturing, or harvesting?
- 10:45 a.m. — Garden walk · agree tasks and work with a lead
- 12:30 p.m. — Check-in · wash up and share lunch
- 12:50 p.m. — Reflection · share discoveries and ideas
- 1:40 p.m. — Return tools · tidy up together
- 2:00 p.m. — Closing · agree care tasks and the next connection

## Role plan

- Event lead: 1 requested. Confirm priorities, welcome the group, and coordinate the day. Draft lead nickname: Demo owl.
- Space & tools: 1 requested. Prepare the space, tools, materials, and arrival signs with the host.
- Welcome crew: 1 requested. Help with nametags, sign-in, and finding the garden lead.
- Comfort & care: 2 requested. Prepare water, snacks, and the shared meal with the host.
- Garden team: 8 requested. Work on the agreed tasks with an experienced garden lead.
- Cleanup crew: 2 requested. Return tools and help leave the space ready for its neighbors.
- Notes & next steps: 1 requested. Capture ideas from the closing discussion and agreed follow-up.

## Before the day

- [ ] Confirm the host, tasks, and public arrival details.

- [ ] Review the host’s safety and participation arrangements.
